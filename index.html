01 — About

Educator. Trainer.
Innovator.

Bringing technology and scholarship together to inspire the next generation.

Muhammad Asadullah is an Assistant Professor at Syed Shah Mostafa College in Moulvibazar, where he teaches Al-Hadith and Islamic Studies. Beyond the classroom, he has distinguished himself as a Master Trainer of Trainers and a pioneering ICT educator in the secondary and higher secondary education system of Bangladesh.

He serves as the ICT4E District Ambassador for Moulvibazar at the national Teacher's Portal, and was recognised as a Microsoft Innovative Expert (MIE) in 2017. He combines deep Islamic scholarship with a passion for digital skills, making him a unique bridge between tradition and technology.
